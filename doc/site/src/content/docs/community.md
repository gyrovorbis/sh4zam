The `#sh4zam` channel within the [Simulant Discord Server](https://discord.gg/G2Ay9kxec2) for Dreamcast development is the best place to ask questions, get help, give feedback, or discuss anything relating to SH4ZAM. Also, feel free to [open tickets](https://github.com/gyrovorbis/sh4zam/issues) for any issues or feature requests.

## Acknowledgements

SH4ZAM began as a collection of inline assembly routines developed specifically to accelerate the math in the Grand Theft Auto 3 and Vice City DC ports. Those routines, along with many others from various contributors, were separated and combined into a single standalone library.
